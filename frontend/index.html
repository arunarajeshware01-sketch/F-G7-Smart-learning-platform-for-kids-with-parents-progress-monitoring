How it works

From sign-up to Sunday's report,
in four steps.

01 · Parent

Register & link

Parent creates an account and links it to their child's profile using the child's details.

02 · Student

Pick a class & subject

Child logs in, selects their class, and picks a subject to start today's lesson.

03 · Student

Learn, watch, quiz

Animated lessons and videos are followed by a short practice quiz.

04 · Parent

Review the report

Scores and time-on-learning roll into daily, weekly and monthly reports.
